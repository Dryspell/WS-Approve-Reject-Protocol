# The Vote Exchange: Grand Strategy Edition

## Overview

A complex strategy board game that combines the minority voting mechanics of The Vote Exchange with colony-building, resource management, and laborer control. This edition brings the full depth of the digital game to the tabletop, ideal for dedicated gaming groups.

**Players:** 3-6
**Play Time:** 2-4 hours
**Age:** 16+

## Design Philosophy

This edition prioritizes:

1. **Faithful Adaptation**: Capturing the strategic depth of laborers, resources, and voting
2. **Manageable Complexity**: Streamlining where necessary for physical play
3. **Social Dynamics**: Emphasizing negotiation and table-talk
4. **Deterministic Combat**: Using card-based combat for quick resolution
5. **Modular Scaling**: Rules that work from 3 players to 6

**Key Differences from Digital:**
- Fixed round structure (no real-time)
- Simplified resource tiers
- Card-based combat instead of simulation
- Sticky note communication instead of digital chat
- Combined trading phase (resources + votes simultaneous)

## Prototyping Approach

This section documents the physical prototyping strategy using accessible crafting tools.

### Currency: US Coins

Instead of custom marble tokens, use standard US coins:

| Coin | Value (Marbles) | Notes |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| Penny | 1 | Copper color distinguishes from higher values |
| Nickel | 5 | |
| Dime | 10 | Small size, high value |
| Quarter | 25 | For large transactions |

**Per Player Starting Amount (20 Marbles):**
- 10 pennies, 2 nickels = 20¢ worth of coins
- Or: 4 nickels = 20¢
- Recommend coin trays/dishes per player

**Pot Coins:** Collect in central bowl or tray.

### Communication: Sticky Notes

Replace digital chat and whisper mechanics with physical sticky note pads:

**Setup:**
- Each player receives a small sticky note pad (different colors optional)
- Notes can be passed openly or secretly

**Mechanics:**
- Write offers, guarantees, or messages on notes
- Pass to specific players or place face-down for them to retrieve
- **Obfuscation allowed**: Players may write decoy notes, pass empty notes, or write unrelated messages to confuse opponents
- Notes should be destroyed (crumpled/discarded) after reading to prevent later reference
- No verbal whispering required—all private communication via notes

**Strategic Depth:**
- Passing many notes (even blank ones) creates uncertainty
- Decoy trades can mislead observers about your intentions
- Note-passing timing itself becomes a signal

### Laborer Figurines: 3D Printed with Swappable Platforms

**Design:**
- Small figurines (~25mm tall) representing laborers
- Each figurine stands on a colored platform/base
- Platform color indicates current owner

**Ownership Transfer:**
- When a laborer's vote is sold, swap the platform to buyer's color
- Physical swap makes ownership visible to all players
- Original owner retains the laborer card (stats/equipment)

**Platform Attachment Options:**
- Friction fit (simple, may wobble)
- Magnetic (secure, requires magnets in both pieces)
- Slot/peg system (most stable)

**Production Plan:**
- Figurine bodies: 60 total (one per laborer card)
- Platforms: 6 per player color × 6 players = 36 platforms
- Test batch first to verify mechanism

### Building Tiles & Cards: Cricut Cut

**Building Tiles:**
- Cut from heavy cardstock or chipboard
- Hexagonal shape for colony grid placement
- Artwork printed, then cut to shape
- Consider lamination for durability

**Cards (Laborer, Equipment, Contract):**
- Standard card size for sleeving
- Print on cardstock, Cricut cut for clean edges
- Alternatively: home print + purchase blank cards

**Player Boards:**
- Larger Cricut cuts from chipboard
- Design with resource track grooves
- Consider dry-erase laminate overlay

## Game Board Overview

The main board is a simple **Village Map** with:

1. **Resource Locations**: 4 areas (Forest, Quarry, Mine, Farm) where laborers gather
2. **The Market**: Central area for trading
3. **Player Zones**: Seating areas around the table (combat flows clockwise)
4. **The Pot**: Central dish for collected coins

No complex hex grid needed—the village IS the arena.

1. **Board Setup:**
   - Place the Village Map centrally (or just designate table areas)
   - Place the Pot (empty bowl/dish) in center
   - Set out resource cubes near their locations

2. **Player Setup (each player receives):**
   - 1 Player Screen
   - **5 Starting Laborer Figurines** (on their colored platforms)
   - **5 Starting Resources** (player's choice of Wood/Stone/Ore/Food)
   - 20 Marbles (coins) for wallet
   - 1 Sticky Note Pad
   - Vote Tokens (5 to match laborers)

3. **Central Pool:**
   - Form the Pot with **10 Marbles per player** (e.g., 60 Marbles for 6 players)
   - Stack any Equipment cards nearby

## Game Length & Balance Considerations

**Expected Rounds:**

Since laborers voting in the majority are eliminated (or fight), the laborer count roughly halves each round. Expected game length:

| Starting Laborers (Total) | Expected Rounds |
|---------------------------|-----------------|
| 30 (6 players × 5) | ~5 rounds |
| 48 (6 players × 8) | ~6 rounds |
| 60 (6 players × 10) | ~6-7 rounds |

**Tuning Options:**
- **More laborers** = longer game, more trading opportunities
- **Laborer recruitment** = players can buy new laborers mid-game
- **Partial elimination** = only some majority laborers fight (e.g., excess over minority count)

*Start with 5 laborers for first playtest, adjust based on feel.*

## Game Structure

The game plays in **Rounds** until a win condition is met. No era system—just continuous rounds.

Each Round follows **3 phases**:

1. **Action Phase** (Production + Trading simultaneously)
2. **Voting**
3. **Combat & Resolution**

---

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Action Phase (5-10 minutes)

**Everything happens simultaneously!** Players produce resources AND trade at the same time.

**Production:**
- Place laborer figurines at resource locations to gather
- Each laborer at a location collects **1 resource** of that type
- Take the resource cube immediately
- Laborers can be moved/reassigned freely during this phase

**Trading (Simultaneous):**
While production happens, trading occurs freely:

*Public Trading (Verbal):*
- "Selling 3 Wood for 5 Marbles!"
- "Vote for sale, 8 Marbles!"
- First to accept gets the deal

*Private Trading (Sticky Notes):*
- Pass notes for secret offers, guarantees, counter-offers
- **Decoy notes allowed** for obfuscation
- Destroy notes after reading

**Vote Sales:**
- When a vote is sold, buyer swaps their colored platform onto the laborer figurine
- This makes ownership change visible to all
- Original owner keeps the laborer for production purposes

**Guarantees:**
- Agree on guarantee → fill out Contract Card
- Private: fold card so only parties see
- Public: display openly

**Phase Ends:**
- When timer expires (recommend 5-10 min based on player count)
- OR when all players agree to move on
- Incomplete trades are voided

### Phase 2: Voting

1. **Assignment:** Each player assigns their laborers (and any controlled votes) to Red or Blue
   - Place Vote Tokens behind your screen
   - Match each token to a specific laborer figurine

2. **Reveal:** On the count of three, all players reveal their color assignments

3. **Tally:** Count total Red vs. Blue votes

4. **Determine Majority:** The color with more votes is majority
   - **Tie:** Pot splits proportionally, game ends (or continues if players agree)

### Phase 3: Combat & Resolution

**Clockwise Combat** (Attack Player to Your Left):

Instead of a complex battle arena, combat flows around the table:

1. **Identify Combatants:** All laborers who voted with the **majority** must fight
2. **Combat Direction:** Each player's majority-voting laborers attack the player to their **left** who also has majority-voting laborers
3. **Resolution:** Compare total Combat Values:
   - **Attacker Total** = Sum of Attack stats of your majority laborers
   - **Defender Total** = Sum of Defense stats of their majority laborers
   - If Attacker ≥ Defender: Defender loses laborers equal to the difference (min 1)
   - If Defender > Attacker: Attacker loses 1 laborer

4. **Simultaneous Resolution:** All combat pairs resolve at the same time (use original stats, not post-combat)

5. **Remove Casualties:** Eliminated laborers (and their platforms) are removed from the game

**Example (4 players, clockwise seating A→B→C→D):**
- A has 2 majority laborers (Attack 5), B has 3 (Defense 4), C has 1, D has 0
- A attacks B: 5 vs 4 → B loses 1 laborer
- B attacks C: B's attack vs C's defense
- C attacks D: D has no majority laborers, so C's attack goes to next player with majority (wraps to A)
- Players with no majority laborers are skipped

**If Only One Player Has Majority Laborers:**
- No combat occurs—those laborers are simply eliminated (they voted majority alone)

**Contract Resolution:**
- Check all guarantee contracts
- Fulfilled: Seller keeps payment
- Broken: Seller pays **double** back

**Prepare Next Round:**
- Return vote tokens to players
- Sold laborers return to original owners (swap platforms back)
- Announce round number, continue

## The Laborer System

### Laborer Figurines

Each laborer is represented by:
- **Figurine**: 3D printed (~25mm tall)
- **Platform**: Colored base indicating current vote owner
- **Stats** (optional): Attack 1-3, Defense 1-3 (written on platform or card)

For simpler play, all laborers can have identical stats (Attack 2, Defense 2).

### Laborer Placement

During the Action Phase, place laborers at resource locations:

| Location | Produces |
|----------|----------|
| Forest | 1 Wood per laborer |
| Quarry | 1 Stone per laborer |
| Mine | 1 Metal Ore per laborer |
| Farm | 1 Food per laborer |

Laborers not placed at locations don't produce but can still be traded/sold.

### Voting with Laborers

- Each laborer = 1 Vote
- **Platform color** indicates who controls the vote (not who "owns" the laborer)
- Sold laborers vote under buyer's control
- Guaranteed laborers must vote as promised (or pay penalty)
- Players with multiple votes may split between Red and Blue

### Ownership vs Vote Control

This is a key concept:
- **Owner**: The player who started with the laborer (for production purposes)
- **Vote Controller**: The player whose platform color is on the figurine
- When votes are sold, only vote control transfers—not the laborer itself
- After voting resolves, sold laborers return to original owners (swap platforms back)

## Market Mechanics

### The Order Board

A physical board with dry-erase sections:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│           BUY ORDERS                │
│  [Wood: 3M] [Stone: 4M] [Ore: 5M]  │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│          SELL ORDERS                │
│  [Wood: 5M] [Ingot: 8M] [Sword: 15M]│
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│         VOTE TRADES                 │
│  [Vote: 8M] [Red Guarantee: 4M]    │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Resource Trading

- Post orders on your Order Slate
- Place in central Order Board area
- Others accept by calling "Trade!" and pointing
- Execute immediately

### Vote Trading

**Vote Sales:**
- Seller gives buyer control of one laborer's vote
- Laborer card stays with original owner
- Buyer places a Vote Token on the card as marker

**Vote Buybacks:**
- Original owner may "buyback" before voting
- Must pay 150% of original sale price
- Buyer cannot refuse

### Guarantee Contracts

**Public Guarantees:**
1. Announce: "I offer a [Red/Blue] guarantee for [X] Marbles"
2. First taker pays and receives Contract Card
3. Card states: "Player A guarantees to vote [Color]"

**Private Guarantees:**
1. Whisper offer to specific player(s)
2. If accepted, fill out Contract Card secretly
3. Only revealed during Resolution if relevant

**Breaking Guarantees:**
- If laborer votes against guarantee: Pay 2× Marbles back
- If laborer is sold after guarantee: Guarantee transfers to buyer

## Combat System (Clockwise Attack)

Combat is simple: **majority voters attack the player to their left**.

### Why Clockwise?

- No complex positioning or tactical movement
- Creates natural alliances and rivalries based on seating
- Fast resolution—no separate "arena" needed
- The village IS the battlefield

### Combat Stats

Each laborer has simple stats:
- **Attack**: Damage dealt when attacking (typically 1-3)
- **Defense**: Damage absorbed when defending (typically 1-3)

Equipment can modify these values.

### Resolution Summary

1. All majority-voting laborers fight
2. Your laborers attack the player to your left (who also has majority laborers)
3. Compare totals: Attacker's Attack vs Defender's Defense
4. Difference = casualties for the loser (minimum 1 if attacker wins)
5. All combats resolve simultaneously

### Edge Cases

- **No one to attack**: If the player to your left has no majority laborers, skip to the next player clockwise who does
- **Only you have majority laborers**: Your laborers are eliminated with no combat (voted alone in majority)
- **Tie in combat totals**: Both sides lose 1 laborer each

### Casualties

- Remove eliminated laborer figurines and platforms from the game
- Equipment on eliminated laborers returns to supply (or is discarded)

**Empty Marbles (MT)** are the game's currency.

- Starting amount: 20 per player
- Additional Marbles enter through:
  - Pot distribution (game end)
  - Selling resources/laborers
  - Building income

**Bank:**
- Unlimited Marbles available from supply
- Players may store Marbles behind screen

## Winning the Game

**Primary Victory: Last Standing**

The game ends when one of these occurs:

1. **Solo Survivor**: One player remains with laborers → **wins the entire pot**
2. **Two Players Remain**: 
   - If one controls more votes, they can split to guarantee minority
   - If tied 1-1, they vote and split the pot (or play sudden death)
3. **Tie in Voting**: Red = Blue votes → pot splits proportionally by votes controlled

**Alternative: Point Victory (Optional)**

If players prefer a definite endpoint, play for X rounds (e.g., 6 rounds), then score:

| Scoring Element | Points |
|-----------------|--------|
| Per surviving Laborer | 3 VP |
| Per Equipment Card | 1 VP |
| Per 10 Marbles | 1 VP |
| Pot Share | 1 VP per 5 Marbles |

Highest VP wins. Ties split victory.

**Domination Victory:**

If one player controls **75%+ of all laborers** after any round → immediate victory + full pot

## Side-Bets (Spectator Participation)

Players who have lost all their laborers (or sold all their votes) can remain engaged through side-bets.

### How Side-Bets Work

**Betting Window:**
- During the Trading phase, eliminated players may place side-bets
- Write bet on a sticky note: "5 Marbles on Red majority" or "3 Marbles on tie"
- Place bet with Marbles in a separate side-pot area

**Bet Types:**

| Bet | Payout | Description |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Majority Color | 2:1 | Bet which color wins majority |
| Tie | 5:1 | Bet on exact tie |
| Specific Count | 3:1 | Bet exact vote count (e.g., "Red wins 7-4") |
| Player Survives | 2:1 | Bet a specific player has no eliminations |
| Player Eliminated | 2:1 | Bet a specific player loses laborers |

**Resolution:**
- After voting phase, resolve all side-bets
- Winning bets paid from side-pot
- Losing bets remain in side-pot
- Unclaimed side-pot carries to next round

### Re-Entry via Side-Bets

**Optional Rule:** If an eliminated player accumulates enough Marbles through side-bets:
- May purchase a new laborer from the deck for 15 Marbles
- Receives a fresh platform in their color
- Returns to active play next round
- Creates comeback potential and maintains engagement

### Side-Bet Etiquette

- Eliminated players should not verbally influence active players
- Side-bets are placed silently via sticky notes
- Active players may ignore or consider side-bet information strategically

## Variant Rules

### Quick Play (30-45 minutes)

- Start with **3 laborers** each
- No buildings, no equipment
- 3-minute Action Phase
- Pure voting and trading focus

### Extended Play (2+ hours)

- Start with **8-10 laborers** each
- Include upgrade buildings
- Include equipment cards
- Allow laborer recruitment mid-game (15 Marbles = 1 new laborer)

### Eternal Format (Campaign)

For groups wanting ongoing play:

- Game continues across sessions
- Players may join/leave between sessions
- Pot distributes 50% each session, 50% carries forward
- Track player Marble totals between games

### Partial Elimination Variant

Instead of all majority laborers fighting:

- Only **excess** majority laborers fight
- Example: 8 Red vs 5 Blue → only 3 Red laborers (8-5) enter combat
- Slows elimination rate, extends game length
- Good for learning games

### Solo/Cooperative Mode

Play against an automated "Shadow Syndicate":

- Shadow has 10 laborers, votes randomly (flip coin for each)
- Players cooperate to out-vote the Shadow
- Victory: Eliminate all Shadow laborers before it dominates

## Physical Adaptations

### Hidden Information Solutions

**Private Holdings:**
- Player screens hide: Marbles, vote assignments, unplaced laborers
- Equipment on laborers is public information

**Secret Deals:**
- Use the Whisper Corner for private negotiations
- Written notes may be passed (destroyed after reading)
- No electronic communication allowed

**Vote Hiding:**
- Assign votes behind screen using Vote Tokens placed on laborer cards
- Reveal simultaneously when called

### Simultaneous Actions

**Production Phase:**
- All players act simultaneously
- Use the timer to keep phase moving
- No player may observe another's player board during production

**Market Phase:**
- Orders are placed simultaneously on slates
- Then all slates revealed together
- Acceptance is real-time (first caller wins)

### Timing Solutions

**Sand Timers:**
- 5-minute: Standard Action Phase
- 10-minute: Extended Action Phase (for more players)

**Flexible Timing:**
- Play until activity naturally ceases
- Any player can call "1 minute warning"
- All players must agree to move on early
