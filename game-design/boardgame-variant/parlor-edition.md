# The Vote Exchange: Parlor Edition

## Overview

A fast-paced social deduction and negotiation board game for 4-10 players. This streamlined variant captures the essence of The Vote Exchange's minority voting and trading mechanics while adapting them for smooth in-person play.

**Players:** 4-10
**Play Time:** 30-60 minutes
**Age:** 14+

## Components

- **Vote Tokens** (2 per player): Red side and Blue side
- **Money Chips**: Denominations of $1, $5, $10, $25
- **Vote Ownership Markers**: Colored discs matching player colors
- **Contract Cards**: For recording guarantee agreements
- **Sand Timer**: 5-minute timer for negotiation phase
- **Vote Tray**: Slotted tray for hidden simultaneous voting
- **Player Screens**: Small folding screens to hide money and tokens
- **Round Tracker Board**
- **Reference Cards**: One per player with rules summary

---

## Setup

1. Each player receives:
   - 1 Vote Token (starts with voting rights)
   - Starting money based on player count:
     - 4-6 players: $20 each
     - 7-10 players: $15 each
   - 1 Player Screen
   - 1 Reference Card

2. Place buy-in money ($5 per player) in the center to form **The Pot**

3. Give each player a Vote Ownership Marker matching their color

4. Shuffle Contract Cards and place within reach

5. Set the Sand Timer ready

---

## Game Flow

Each round consists of three phases:

### Phase 1: Negotiation (5 minutes)

Flip the sand timer. During this time, players may:

- **Announce** intentions publicly (non-binding)
- **Whisper** to other players for private deals
- **Trade** vote tokens for money
- **Offer/Accept Guarantees**

**Trading Areas:**
- Players may form small groups for private negotiations
- The "Market Corner" is a designated area where players can openly post asking/offering prices on small whiteboards or price cards
- Deals are final once money changes hands

**When the Timer Runs Out:**
- All negotiations must cease immediately
- Any incomplete trades are voided
- Players return to their seats

### Phase 2: Voting

1. All players with Vote Tokens place them face-down in the Vote Tray, showing their chosen color (Red or Blue) hidden underneath

2. Players who sold their votes sit out this phase

3. Once all votes are in, the Dealer calls "Reveal!"

4. All tokens are flipped simultaneously

### Phase 3: Resolution

1. **Count the Votes**: Total Red vs. Blue

2. **Determine Majority**: The color with more votes is the majority
   - **Tie**: See Tie Rules below

3. **Eliminate Majority Voters**: All players whose vote tokens show the majority color are eliminated from the game
   - Return their tokens to the supply
   - They keep any money earned but cannot win the pot

4. **Minority Voters Advance**: Remaining players keep their tokens and proceed to the next round

5. **Clean Up Guarantees**: 
   - Players who fulfilled guarantees keep their payment
   - Players who broke guarantees must pay back double (if able)

## Trading Mechanics

### Vote Sales

**Selling Your Vote:**
1. Agree on a price with a buyer
2. Hand your Vote Token to the buyer
3. Receive payment immediately
4. You are now out of the voting phase (but not eliminated)
5. You may re-enter via Rebuy if playing with that variant

**Buying a Vote:**
1. You now control multiple Vote Tokens
2. You may split votes between Red and Blue to guarantee minority status
3. Additional votes carry over to future rounds

**Split Voting Rule:**
If you control 2+ votes, you MAY split them between Red and Blue. However:
- You cannot split a single token
- Splitting guarantees at least one vote survives

### Guarantees (Simplified)

Guarantees are promises to vote a specific way.

**Public Guarantee:**
1. Announce publicly: "I guarantee I will vote [Red/Blue] for $X"
2. Any player may accept and pay you
3. You take a Contract Card and write the agreement
4. Both parties sign (or place ownership markers on the card)
5. If you vote correctly: Keep the money
6. If you break the guarantee: Pay back the money plus a penalty equal to the original amount

**Private Guarantee:**
1. Whisper your offer to specific players
2. Same mechanics, but the agreement is secret
3. Only the involved parties know about it
4. This allows for bluffing and information asymmetry

## Handling Hidden Information

### Private Negotiations

**The Whisper Rule:**
- Players may leave the table briefly (within view) for private deals
- Whispered conversations at the table are allowed
- Other players may NOT demand to know deal contents
- Bluffing about deals is explicitly allowed

**Written Deals:**
- Players may write deals on Contract Cards secretly
- Fold the card so only parties involved can see
- Reveals happen only during Resolution if relevant

### Public Announcements

Any player may make public announcements:
- "I'm voting Red!" (may be a bluff)
- "I'll sell my vote for $8!"
- "I'm buying votes at $5 each!"

Public statements are NEVER binding unless a Contract Card is used.

## Winning the Game

**Game End Conditions:**

1. **Single Winner**: One player remains with a vote
   - That player wins the entire pot

2. **Two Players Remain**: If only two players have votes
   - If one has multiple votes: They split to guarantee minority and win
   - If each has one vote: Final tiebreaker round (see below)

3. **Tie in Voting**: If Red and Blue votes are exactly equal
   - The pot is split among all remaining voters proportional to votes controlled
   - Game ends

**Final Tiebreaker (Two Players, One Vote Each):**
- Both players vote simultaneously
- If they vote different colors: They split the pot evenly
- If they vote the same color: Both are eliminated, pot carries to next game (or is split evenly by house rule)

## Optional Rules

### Rebuy Variant

**For Longer Play Sessions:**

- Eliminated players may rebuy into the next round
- Rebuy cost: 3× the original buy-in
- Rebuy money goes into the pot
- Rebuying player starts with 1 vote token

### Side Bets

**Spectator Engagement:**

- Eliminated players may bet on round outcomes
- Create a separate side pot for spectator bets
- Side bets are settled before the main pot

### Bluffing Guarantees

**High Risk, High Reward:**

- If you break a guarantee but end up eliminated anyway, you owe nothing
- If you break a guarantee and survive, you owe triple instead of double
- Creates tension around "guarantee insurance"

---

## Strategy Tips

1. **Early Rounds**: Information is scarce. Selling your vote early can be profitable but removes your agency.

2. **The Guarantee Game**: Buying guarantees provides information, but remember—people can lie.

3. **Vote Splitting**: Accumulating multiple votes is powerful. Splitting guarantees survival but dilutes payout.

4. **The Bluff**: Publicly announcing one color while voting the opposite is risky but can manipulate others.

5. **Read the Room**: Watch for alliances forming. Sometimes breaking a deal is worth the penalty.

## Example Round

**Setup**: 6 players (Alice, Bob, Charlie, Diana, Eve, Frank) with $30 pot.

**Negotiation Phase:**
- Frank announces: "Selling my vote for $8!"
- Alice whispers to Bob: "Want to buy Frank's vote together? We split it."
- Charlie loudly offers: "I'll pay $4 for any Blue guarantee!"
- Diana takes Charlie's offer privately and signs a contract
- Eve buys Frank's vote for $7 (outbidding others)

**Vote Phase:**
- Eve now has 2 votes (her own + Frank's)
- Frank has no vote (sits out)
- Remaining voters: Alice, Bob, Charlie, Diana, Eve (with 2)

**Votes Revealed:**
- Alice: Blue
- Bob: Red
- Charlie: Red
- Diana: Blue (per her guarantee)
- Eve: Red + Blue (split)

**Result:**
- Red: 3 (Bob, Charlie, Eve's 1)
- Blue: 3 (Alice, Diana, Eve's 1)
- **TIE!**

**Resolution:**
- Pot is split among all voters proportionally
- 6 votes total, $30 pot = $5 per vote
- Eve gets $10 (2 votes), others get $5 each
- Game ends
